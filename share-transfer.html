Share Transfer and Transmission Services in India

Professional share transfer documentation, FC-TRS filing for cross-border transfers, share transmission, and corporate actions advisory for Indian companies.

Overview

Expert Share Transfer and Corporate Actions Advisory

Share transfer is a fundamental corporate action involving the transfer of ownership of shares from one person to another. In India, share transfers are governed by the Companies Act 2013, the Articles of Association of the company, and for cross-border transactions, the Foreign Exchange Management Act (FEMA) and SEBI regulations. The process requires careful compliance with statutory requirements including board approval, share transfer deed execution, stamp duty payment, register of members update, and share certificate issuance. For companies with foreign shareholding or those involving non-resident parties, additional FEMA compliance including FC-TRS filing with the Reserve Bank of India is mandatory.

Non-compliance with share transfer requirements can result in penalties under the Companies Act, invalidation of the transfer, dispute with shareholders, and regulatory action from the ROC or RBI. For cross-border transfers, non-compliance with FEMA can attract penalties up to three times the sum involved in the contravention. Given the complexities involved in share transfers, particularly those involving cross-border elements, promoter changes, or special category shares, professional advisory support is essential to ensure smooth execution and full compliance.

Our services include share transfer deed preparation, board resolution drafting and approval, stamp duty computation and e-stamping, FC-TRS filing for cross-border transfers, register of members updates, share certificate issuance, and ROC filings where applicable. We work closely with company secretaries, registrars, and transfer agents to ensure seamless execution of share transfers while maintaining full compliance with all applicable laws.

Share Transfer Process

Share Transfer Process and Documentation

A systematic approach to executing share transfers in compliance with the Companies Act, SEBI, and FEMA regulations.

01

Transfer Application and Board Approval

The process begins with the transferor submitting a share transfer application to the company. The board of directors must review the application and pass a resolution approving the transfer. For private companies, the Articles of Association may contain restrictions on transferability such as pre-emption rights, right of first refusal, or approval requirements. The board must verify compliance with these restrictions before approving the transfer. We assist in drafting board resolutions, reviewing Articles of Association, and ensuring that all procedural requirements are met before the transfer is approved.

02

Transfer Deed Execution and Stamp Duty

The share transfer must be executed using the prescribed share transfer form SH-4 under the Companies Act. The transfer deed must be affixed with appropriate stamp duty as per the Indian Stamp Act and the respective state stamp regulations. Currently stamp duty on share transfer is 0.015% of the market value or consideration value, whichever is higher. We handle the computation of stamp duty, e-stamping through SHCIL, execution of the transfer deed, and ensure that all documentation is complete and valid.

03

Register of Members and Share Certificate

Upon completion of the transfer process, the company must update its register of members to reflect the change in ownership. The company must also issue a new share certificate to the transferee within 15 days from the date of receipt of the share transfer deed, or within such other time as may be prescribed. For listed companies, the transfer must also be processed through the depository system and the beneficial owner records must be updated accordingly.

04

Cross-Border Transfer and FC-TRS Filing

When shares are transferred between a resident and a non-resident, the transaction must comply with FEMA regulations and the FDI policy. The FC-TRS form must be filed within 60 days from the date of transfer through the AD Category-I bank. The transfer must comply with the pricing guidelines for the transfer of shares between residents and non-residents. We handle the complete FC-TRS filing process including documentation, valuation compliance, AD bank coordination, and post-transfer reporting.

Share Transmission

Share Transmission Services

Statutory process of transferring shares to persons entitled by operation of law without requiring a transfer deed.

Transmission on Death

When a shareholder passes away, the shares must be transmitted to the legal heirs or nominees as per the succession laws applicable to the deceased shareholder. If a nomination exists, the company must transfer the shares to the nominee upon receipt of the death certificate and nomination form. In the absence of nomination, the legal heirs must obtain succession certificates or probate of will from the appropriate court. We assist in the complete transmission process including documentation, legal heir verification, and compliance with the Companies Act requirements.

  • Death certificate and succession documentation
  • Nomination verification and processing
  • Succession certificate assistance
  • Register of members update

Other Transmission Cases

Share transmission also occurs in cases of insolvency or bankruptcy of a shareholder, where the Official Receiver or Trustee in Bankruptcy becomes entitled to the shares. In cases of mental incapacity, the Court-appointed guardian or manager may be entitled to the shares. Additionally, in cases of amalgamation or merger, shares may be transmitted as per the scheme of arrangement approved by the NCLT. We handle all categories of share transmission ensuring proper documentation and compliance.

  • Insolvency and bankruptcy transmission
  • Guardianship and mental incapacity
  • Amalgamation and merger transmission
  • NCLT scheme-based transmission
Cross-Border Transfers

Cross-Border Share Transfer and FC-TRS Filing

FEMA-compliant share transfer services for transactions between residents and non-residents including FC-TRS filing and pricing compliance.

FC-TRS Filing Requirements

When shares of an Indian company are transferred between a resident and a non-resident, the FC-TRS form must be filed with the RBI within 60 days from the date of transfer. The form is filed through the AD Category-I bank and requires comprehensive documentation including the transfer deed, board resolution, pricing compliance certificate, and KYC details of both parties. We manage the complete FC-TRS filing process ensuring timely submission and regulatory compliance.

  • FC-TRS form preparation and filing
  • Pricing compliance verification
  • AD bank coordination
  • 60-day deadline management

Pricing Guidelines Compliance

The pricing of shares transferred between residents and non-residents must comply with the FDI pricing guidelines issued by the RBI. For unlisted companies, shares must be transferred at a price not less than the fair market value determined as per internationally accepted pricing methodologies such as the DCF method. For listed companies, SEBI regulations govern the pricing. We assist in determining the appropriate valuation methodology, obtaining the valuation certificate from a registered valuer, and ensuring compliance with the pricing guidelines.

  • DCF valuation for unlisted companies
  • SEBI pricing for listed companies
  • Registered valuer certification
  • Valuation report documentation

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the process for share transfer in a private company?

Share transfer in a private company involves executing a share transfer deed in the prescribed form SH-4, obtaining board approval, updating the register of members, and issuing a new share certificate. The transfer must comply with restrictions on transferability in the Articles of Association. Stamp duty must be paid on the transfer deed.

What is FC-TRS filing?

FC-TRS (Form for reporting foreign currency transaction in transfer of shares) is filed with the RBI when there is a transfer of shares between a resident and a non-resident or vice versa. The form must be filed within 60 days from the date of transfer through the AD Category-I bank. The transfer must comply with the pricing guidelines prescribed under the FDI policy and FEMA regulations.

What is share transmission?

Share transmission is the process of transferring shares to a person who becomes entitled to them by operation of law, such as by inheritance, succession, bankruptcy, or court order. Unlike share transfer, transmission does not require a transfer deed. The company must update its register of members and issue a new share certificate.

What stamp duty is applicable on share transfer?

Stamp duty on share transfer is 0.015% of the market value of the shares or consideration value, whichever is higher. The stamp duty must be paid through the SHCIL e-stamping mechanism. Physical stamp paper requirement has been replaced with e-stamping under the latest amendments to the Indian Stamp Act.

Do I need board approval for share transfer?

Yes, board approval is generally required for share transfers in private companies. The board must pass a resolution approving the transfer and authorizing the allotment of shares to the transferee. For listed companies, SEBI regulations govern the share transfer process and the transfer must be approved by the Share Transfer Committee.
