Business Structures

Types of Companies in India

India offers several business structures under the Companies Act 2013 and the LLP Act 2008, each suited for different business objectives.

Private Limited Company (Pvt Ltd)

The most popular business structure for startups and growth-oriented businesses in India. A Private Limited Company is held by a minimum of 2 and maximum of 200 members. It offers limited liability protection, separate legal entity status, and is the preferred structure for raising equity funding from investors.

  • Minimum 2 directors, maximum 15
  • No minimum paid-up capital requirement
  • Restricts share transferability
  • Ideal for VC/angel funding

Public Limited Company (Ltd)

A Public Limited Company can be listed on a stock exchange and allows public subscription of shares. It requires a minimum of 7 members and 3 directors, making it suitable for larger enterprises planning to raise capital from the general public through IPOs.

  • Minimum 7 members, no maximum limit
  • Minimum 3 directors
  • Can list on stock exchanges
  • Higher compliance requirements

One Person Company (OPC)

Introduced by the Companies Act 2013, OPC allows a single individual to incorporate a company with limited liability protection. It is ideal for solo entrepreneurs who want the benefits of a corporate structure without the need for multiple members or directors.

  • Single member and director
  • Nominee director required
  • Limited liability protection
  • Only for Indian residents

Limited Liability Partnership (LLP)

Governed by the LLP Act 2008, an LLP combines the flexibility of a partnership with the limited liability benefits of a company. It is suitable for professional services firms, consulting businesses, and entities that do not require external equity funding.

  • Minimum 2 designated partners
  • Lighter compliance burden
  • No concept of share capital
  • Cannot raise equity funding
Our Process

Step-by-Step Incorporation Process

We follow a systematic and transparent approach to ensure your company is registered efficiently and in full compliance with all regulatory requirements.

01

Consultation & Structure Selection

We begin with a detailed consultation to understand your business objectives, funding plans, tax considerations, and compliance preferences. Based on this analysis, we recommend the most suitable business structure — whether it is a Private Limited Company, OPC, LLP, or Public Limited Company.

02

Name Approval & Reservation

We conduct a name availability check on the MCA portal and apply for name reservation through the RUN (Reserve Unique Name) service. We ensure the proposed name complies with the Companies (Incorporation) Rules and does not infringe on any registered trademarks or existing company names.

03

Digital Signature & DIN Application

We assist in obtaining Digital Signature Certificates (DSC) for all proposed directors and apply for Director Identification Numbers (DIN) through the MCA portal. DSC is required for digitally signing incorporation documents, while DIN is a unique identification number for every director.

04

Document Preparation

Our team drafts the Memorandum of Association (MOA) and Articles of Association (AOA) tailored to your business objectives. We also prepare all supporting documents including affidavits, declarations, registered office address proofs, and consent forms from proposed directors.

05

SPICe+ Form Filing

We file the SPICe+ (Simplified Proforma for Incorporating Company Electronically Plus) form on the MCA portal, which is an integrated form for company incorporation, DIN allotment, PAN, TAN, EPFO, ESIC, and GST registration. This single-form approach streamlines the entire incorporation process.

06

Certificate of Incorporation & Post-Incorporation

Upon approval by the Registrar of Companies, we receive the Certificate of Incorporation along with the company's PAN and TAN. We then assist with post-incorporation requirements including opening a bank account, obtaining GST registration, and setting up initial compliance frameworks.

Documentation

Documents Required for Incorporation

The following documents are generally required for company incorporation in India. Specific requirements may vary based on the type of company, number of directors, and state of incorporation.

For Directors & Shareholders

  • PAN Card (mandatory for Indian nationals)
  • Aadhaar Card
  • Passport (for foreign nationals)
  • Address proof (utility bill, bank statement)
  • Passport-size photograph
  • Specimen signature for DIN

For Registered Office

  • Property ownership proof or rental agreement
  • NOC from property owner
  • Utility bill (electricity, water, telephone)
  • Land records (for owned property)
Post-Incorporation

Compliance Requirements After Incorporation

Once your company is incorporated, several ongoing compliance obligations must be fulfilled to maintain good standing with the Registrar of Companies.

Annual Filings

Annual return (MGT-7), financial statements (AOC-4), and annual compliance filings with the ROC within prescribed due dates.

Board Meetings

Minimum of 4 board meetings per year with proper notice, agenda, minutes, and quorum compliance as per the Companies Act 2013.

Director KYC

Annual DIR-3 KYC filing for every director and designated partner to update personal and contact information with the MCA.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

How long does it take to register a private limited company in India?

Company incorporation typically takes 10-15 working days from the date of filing all documents with the MCA. The timeline may vary depending on MCA processing times and completeness of documentation.

What is the minimum capital requirement for a private limited company?

There is no minimum paid-up capital requirement for a private limited company in India after the Companies Amendment Act, 2015. You can start with any amount of authorized and paid-up capital.

Can a foreign national be a director in an Indian company?

Yes, a foreign national can be a director in an Indian company. However, at least one director must be a resident of India (staying in India for at least 182 days in the previous financial year).

What documents are required for company incorporation?

Documents required include: PAN card and Aadhaar of directors, address proof of directors and registered office, identity documents (passport for foreign nationals), MOA and AOA drafts, DIN and DSC of proposed directors, and NOC from the property owner for registered office.

What is the difference between a private limited company and an LLP?

A private limited company has shareholders and directors with limited liability, can raise equity funding, and has stricter compliance requirements. An LLP has partners, is governed by the LLP Act 2008, and has lighter compliance requirements. The choice depends on your business objectives, funding plans, and compliance appetite.

Is DIN required for every director?

Yes, every individual intending to be appointed as a director of a company must obtain a Director Identification Number (DIN). DIN is a unique identification number allotted by the Central Government and is mandatory under Section 153 of the Companies Act 2013.
