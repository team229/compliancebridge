Compliance Calendar

Annual Filing, MSME & PAS-6 — Key Dates

Only the three calendars most clients miss. AGM-linked dates assume AGM on 30 Sep — adjust if your AGM is earlier.

Filing Form Due Date Who Applies
Annual Filing (Companies Act / ROC)
Financial Statements Filing AOC-4 / AOC-4 CFS 30 days from AGM (29 Oct if AGM 30 Sep) All companies
Annual Return Filing MGT-7 / MGT-7A 60 days from AGM (29 Nov if AGM 30 Sep) All companies (7A for OPC / Small Co.)
Auditor Appointment ADT-1 15 days from AGM Companies appointing / re-appointing auditor
MSME (Half-Yearly)
MSME Dues Return (Apr–Sep period) MSME Form I 31 Oct Cos with MSME dues outstanding >45 days
MSME Dues Return (Oct–Mar period) MSME Form I 30 Apr Cos with MSME dues outstanding >45 days
PAS-6 — Reconciliation of Share Capital (Half-Yearly)
Share Capital Reconciliation (Apr–Sep) PAS-6 29 Nov (60 days from half-year end) Unlisted companies with demat shares
Share Capital Reconciliation (Oct–Mar) PAS-6 30 May (60 days from half-year end) Unlisted companies with demat shares

Dates indicative; MCA may extend via circulars. DIR-3 KYC (30 Sep) and DPT-3 (30 Sep) run alongside — covered under Annual Compliance.

FAQs

Frequently asked questions

Short answers. For anything else, talk to an advisor.

What does ComplianceBridge Advisory do?+

Company Secretarial, Corporate Governance and Regulatory Advisory — Companies Act / ROC, FEMA / RBI / FDI / ODI / ECB, NBFC, board advisory, share transfers, labour law and CSR / ESG / BRSR.

Who do you work with?+

Foreign companies entering India, startups, MSMEs / private companies, and NBFCs / board-led companies. See Who We Help for each playbook.

How does an engagement start?+

Consultation, then a written proposal (scope, timeline, fixed fee) within 48 hours. Execution follows with one checklist and milestone updates. See Engagement Model.

What are the must-track dates for Annual filing, MSME I and PAS-6?+

AOC-4: 30 days from AGM. MGT-7: 60 days from AGM. MSME Form I: 31 Oct and 30 Apr. PAS-6: 29 Nov and 30 May. Full table in Compliance Calendar.

Do you handle FEMA / RBI with Companies Act?+

Yes — one team for ROC plus FDI, FC-GPR / FC-TRS, FLA, ECB, ODI, LEI and RBI filings. See FEMA Compliance and RBI Compliance.

How are fees charged?+

Fixed fee quoted in writing before work. Out-of-scope work is flagged before billing. First scoping consultation is free — schedule a call.
