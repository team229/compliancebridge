Privacy Policy

Connect with our team for compliance and governance advisory

This Privacy Policy explains how Compliancebridge Advisory LLP ("ComplianceBridge Advisory", "we", "us") collects, uses and protects personal information when you visit compliancebridgeadvisory.com or contact us.

1. Information we collect

We collect information you provide directly - such as your name, company, email address, phone number and the details of your inquiry when you contact us through this website, email, phone or WhatsApp. We also collect limited technical data through analytics (such as pages visited and device type) to understand how our website is used.

2. How we use your information

We use your information to respond to inquiries, provide advisory services you request, send relevant compliance updates where you have opted in, and maintain our internal records. We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

3. Cookies and analytics

We use Google Analytics (GA4) to understand aggregate website usage. Google Analytics uses cookies; you can opt out by adjusting your browser settings or using Google's browser add-on. We do not use cookies to personally identify you.

4. Disclosure of information

We may share information with our professional advisers, our technology service providers (for example, our website or email hosting providers), or as required by law, regulation or legal process. Where we share data with service providers, they are bound to use it only to provide services to us.

5. Data retention and security

We retain personal information only as long as necessary for the purposes described in this policy or as required by applicable law. We apply reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect your information. No internet transmission is fully secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

6. Your rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information held by us, or withdraw consent previously given. To make a request, contact us at info@compliancebridgeadvisory.com. We will respond within the timeframes prescribed by applicable Indian law, including the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.

7. Contact

Questions about this Privacy Policy can be sent to info@compliancebridgeadvisory.com or to ComplianceBridge Advisory, Nehru Place, New Delhi, India. This policy may be updated from time to time; the current version will always be published on this page.
